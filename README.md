Entrants write a single sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800. The sentence has to stand alone: no words coined after the traveler's time, no list of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their head. A judge model compares the entries two at a time against the judge brief published with the bounty, and the best ranked sentences take the reward pool.

## The sentence

> Imagine one road joining every post office, library, and market on earth, so any letter, book, or song reaches anyone, anywhere, in the blink of an eye.

152 characters, one sentence, every word a thing a person from 1800 already knows: a road, a post office, a library, a market, a letter, a book, a song. One comparison carries the whole picture — the internet as a single road joining all of them, so that anything written or sung can reach anyone, anywhere, at once.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (creating the directory if needed) and prints it. A built-in validation guard refuses to write anything that breaks the gate: empty or whitespace-only text, over 200 characters, or anything other than exactly one sentence.
